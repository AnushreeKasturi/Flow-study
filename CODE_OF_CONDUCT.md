# Code of conduct

Flow is a place to study with strangers, and this repository is a place to build it with them. Both work only if people feel safe showing up. This applies in issues, pull requests, reviews, and anywhere else you speak for the project.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source or to English.
- Disagree about the work, not the person. Explain why, and accept that the answer may still be no.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Take responsibility when you get something wrong, and fix it.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private messages.
- Comments that demean anyone for who they are: gender, sexuality, disability, appearance, ethnicity, nationality, religion, age, or experience level.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as an address or email, without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after being asked to stop, or deliberately derailing a discussion.

## Reporting

Use GitHub's built-in reporting, which goes to GitHub's moderators:

- On a comment, issue, or pull request: the **⋯** menu → **Report content**.
- On a person: their profile → **Block or report** → **Report abuse**.

For a security problem, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or hide comments, lock or close threads, and block people from the repository, temporarily or for good, depending on how serious and how repeated the behaviour is. A single slip usually gets a reminder; harassment gets a block.
